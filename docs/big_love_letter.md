## **Big Love Letter**

### **Main Text (German to English):**

*"Big Love Letter* is a variant for *Love Letter*, in which more than four players can participate. To do this, two *Love Letter* decks are needed, as the usual 16 cards are not enough for large groups. The table on the right shows which cards are used depending on the number of players.

Don't forget: In *Big Love Letter*, the top card of the draw deck will be set aside by the starting player. The end condition changes: After eight rounds, the game ends. Whoever has collected the most hearts wins the game and the title of Princess. More than eight rounds can also be played if a longer game is desired.

*Big Love Letter* wishes you lots of fun!
— Your Pegasus Spiele Team!"
