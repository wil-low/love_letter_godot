Noble family variant

A simple change to infuse skill into the game:

Distribute 1 card to each player. This will be their first hand.

Distribute the cards evenly between players until 4 cards remain in the deck. This will be their noble family. Burn cards as needed until each player has as much cards as possible, every player has the same number of cards, and exactly 4 cards remain in the deck.

You may look at your noble family at any time, but only your active card is considered your hand.

Whenever you must draw, chose any member of your family. You cannot draw from the deck, except with Chancellor(2019).

If for any reason a player is out of the round, reveal all of his remaining cards (They are not considered discarded.)

The round ends when a player is unable to draw a card.

No other changes.

Edit and excpetions for the sake of clarity regarding card counts:

Base game
Burn 1 card

2 Players: distribute 1 card to each player (13 remaining)
Then, distribute 5 more cards to each player (Total burn is 1+2 = 3)

3 players: distribute 1 card to each player (12 remaining)
Then, distribute 4 more cards to each player (Total burn is 1)

4 Players: do not burn, distribute 1 cards to each player (12 remaining)
Then, distribute 3 more cards to each player (Total burn is 0)
